The Beauty of China

China is one of the world's oldest civilizations, with over 5,000 years of continuous history. From the bustling streets of Beijing to the scenic Li River in Guilin, every corner tells a story.

Rubén visited the Forbidden City, Beihai Park, and the Great Wall — discovering that China is not just a destination, it's a life-changing experience.

English Grammar

Type-3 Conditional Sentences

Travel-inspired sentences using the Third Conditional structure — imagining different outcomes from past travel situations.

📚 Structure Reminder

The Type-3 Conditional describes hypothetical situations in the past — things that did NOT happen but could have changed the outcome.

If + had + past participle → would have + past participle
01
🇨🇳 Beijing, China
If Rubén had not met Chuntal, he would have left the Forbidden City without understanding its history.
Si Rubén no hubiera conocido a Chuntal, habría salido de la Ciudad Prohibida sin entender su historia.
02
🇨🇳 Beijing, China
If she had not explained the history, he would have only seen beautiful walls without understanding their significance.
Si ella no hubiera explicado la historia, él solo habría visto paredes hermosas sin entender su significado.
03
🇨🇳 Great Wall, China
If Rubén had arrived earlier, he would have seen the Great Wall at sunrise and taken amazing photos.
Si Rubén hubiera llegado más temprano, habría visto la Gran Muralla al amanecer y tomado fotos increíbles.
04
🇨🇳 Beijing, China
If he had downloaded a guide before visiting, he would have understood the place even better without needing help.
Si hubiera descargado una guía antes de visitar, habría entendido el lugar aún mejor sin necesitar ayuda.
05
🇨🇳 Beihai Park, China
If it had not been cold that morning, Rubén would have walked around the entire park and rented a bicycle.
Si no hubiera hecho frío esa mañana, Rubén habría caminado por todo el parque y alquilado una bicicleta.
06
🇨🇳 Beijing, China
If Rubén had had more money, he would have bought traditional souvenirs for all his family members.
Si Rubén hubiera tenido más dinero, habría comprado recuerdos tradicionales para todos los miembros de su familia.
07
🇯🇵 Tokyo, Japan
If they had visited during cherry blossom season, the parks would have been covered in beautiful pink flowers.
Si hubieran visitado durante la temporada de cerezos, los parques habrían estado cubiertos de hermosas flores rosas.
08
🇫🇷 Paris, France
If Melissa had finished her project on time, she would have joined Rubén on his trip to Beijing.
Si Melissa hubiera terminado su proyecto a tiempo, habría acompañado a Rubén en su viaje a Beijing.
09
🇨🇳 Shanghai, China
If Rubén had practiced Chinese before the trip, he would not have had so many difficulties communicating with locals.
Si Rubén hubiera practicado chino antes del viaje, no habría tenido tantas dificultades para comunicarse con los locales.
10
🇨🇴 Colombia → China

If Melissa finishes her work on time, they will meet in the morning and make a plan. If the weather is bad, they will stay inside and watch a movie or talk. Type-3 Conditional — Reflecting on the Forbidden City

Rubén has completed his visit to the Forbidden City and has left with a big smile on his face. He has discovered fascinating details about Chinese emperors. Before meeting Chuntal, he had spent a long time looking at the buildings without understanding their meaning. If he had not met Chuntal, he would have left the place without learning anything important. If she had not explained the history of each building, he would have only seen beautiful walls without understanding their significance. If he had not asked her questions, he would have missed the most interesting stories of the place.

By the end of the day, Rubén has thought carefully about everything he experienced and has written notes in his travel journal. He had already visited other places in Beijing, but the Forbidden City has been his favorite. If he had planned his visit better, he would have arrived earlier and seen more areas of the palace. If he had downloaded a guide before going, he would have understood the place even better. If Chuntal had not been so kind, he would have had a completely different experience. He has felt truly grateful for everything he learned.

Q&A

Five Places to Visit in China

Answers to questions from the text "Cinco lugares para visitar en China"

🤖
China Travel Assistant
Ask anything about visiting China
Assistant
👋 Hello! I'm here to answer your questions about the five places to visit in China. Ask me anything!
Student
What are the five must-visit places in China?
Assistant
The five essential places are: 🏯 The Forbidden City in Beijing, 🏔️ The Great Wall near Beijing, 🌊 The Li River in Guilin, 🕌 The Terracotta Army in Xi'an, and 🌆 The Bund in Shanghai.
Student
Why is the Forbidden City so important?
Assistant
The Forbidden City was the imperial palace for 24 emperors over nearly 500 years (1420–1912). With 980 buildings and 8,700 rooms, it is the world's largest ancient palace complex and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Rubén was deeply moved by its history! 🏯
Student
What food should you try in China?
Assistant
You must try: 🥟 Dumplings (Jiaozi), 🦆 Peking Duck, 🍜 Hand-pulled Noodles, 🫕 Hot Pot, and 🥗 Dim Sum. Each region has unique flavors — northern China loves wheat-based dishes while southern China prefers rice and seafood!
